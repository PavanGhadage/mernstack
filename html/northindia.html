Welcome to North India

Place name: Manali

Information: Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh, surrounded by snow-capped mountains, pine forests, and rivers. Popular for honeymooners and adventure seekers.

Major Attractions:

  • Rohtang Pass – Snow and scenic mountain views
  • Solang Valley – Adventure sports like skiing and paragliding
  • Hadimba Devi Temple – Ancient wooden temple
  • Mall Road – Shopping and local food

Place name: Red Fort

Information: The Red Fort (Lal Qila) in Delhi was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in the 17th century. Made of red sandstone, it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Major Attractions:

  • Lahori Gate – Main entrance
  • Diwan-i-Aam – Hall of Public Audience
  • Diwan-i-Khas – Hall of Private Audience
  • Sound & Light Show – Evening historical show

Place name: Jammu

Information: Jammu, known as the “City of Temples,” is the gateway to the Vaishno Devi Temple and an important pilgrimage center.

Major Attractions:

  • Raghunath Temple
  • Vaishno Devi Temple
  • Bahu Fort & Bagh-e-Bahu
  • Mubarak Mandi Palace
