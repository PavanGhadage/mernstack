Welcome to Southern India

Place name: Tirupati

Information: Tirupati is a famous pilgrimage city in Andhra Pradesh, known for Sri Venkateswara Temple on Tirumala Hill and a strong spiritual atmosphere.

Major Attractions:

  • Sri Venkateswara Temple (Tirumala)
  • Sri Kapileswara Swamy Temple
  • Talakona Waterfall
  • Chandragiri Fort

Place name: Mysore

Information: Mysore (Mysuru) is famous for its royal heritage, Mysore Palace, Dussehra celebrations, silk sarees, and sandalwood products.

Major Attractions:

  • Mysore Palace
  • Chamundi Hill & Chamundeshwari Temple
  • Brindavan Gardens
  • Mysore Zoo

Place name: Ooty

Information: Ooty (Udhagamandalam) is a hill station in the Nilgiri Hills, Tamil Nadu, known for tea gardens, cool climate, and scenic beauty.

Major Attractions:

  • Ooty Lake
  • Botanical Gardens
  • Doddabetta Peak – Highest point in the Nilgiris
  • Nilgiri Mountain Railway – UNESCO World Heritage Site
